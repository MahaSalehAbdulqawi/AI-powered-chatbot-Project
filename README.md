# AI-Powered Chatbot Project

This project was completed as a group assignment for the Finance subject at the university. It focuses on how AI-powered chatbot technology can be applied in a business and finance context, using the concepts studied in the course.

## Project Overview

This repository contains the materials for our AI in Business / MIS272 assignment. The aim of the project is to explore the role of AI, particularly chatbots, in supporting business functions, improving customer interaction, and contributing to decision-making in a practical and academic setting.

## Purpose

- Understand the use of AI in business environments
- Explore chatbot applications in finance-related and business contexts
- Apply AI concepts to a real-world project scenario
- Present the findings and assignment work in a clear, organized format
